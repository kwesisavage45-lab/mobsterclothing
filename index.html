Obuasi, Ghana

Mobster Clothing was born in Obuasi, Ghana in 2024 — a brand forged from the streets and built for those who move differently. Each piece is designed with intention, attitude, and a silent power that speaks without words.

Our Philosophy

We create clothing at the intersection of street culture and bold expression. Sharp cuts, heavyweight fabrics, and a palette rooted in power — offset by details that demand a second look.

Made in Ghana

Every garment is produced locally in Ghana with pride and precision. We believe in clothing that carries weight — pieces that command respect and accumulate legacy.
